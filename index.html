A little about me

I’m a Predoc Researcher at TU Wien, where I began my current role in September 2026.

My background is in computer engineering at the University of Tehran. My research experience spans machine-checked proofs in Lean, interpolation-based model checking, and static analysis of reactive systems.

I enjoy working at the intersection of theory and implementation: taking a precise idea and turning it into a tool or proof that helps us understand a system.
